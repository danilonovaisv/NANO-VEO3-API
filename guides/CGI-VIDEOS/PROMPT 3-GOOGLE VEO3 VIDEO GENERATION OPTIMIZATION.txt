VEO3 OPTIMIZATION PRINCIPLES
1. SUBJECT SPECIFICITY

Use concrete, detailed descriptions of people, objects, or entities
Avoid generic terms; be specific about characteristics
Multiple subjects require clear differentiation and positioning

2. ACTION CLARITY

Focus on verbs and adverbs rather than static descriptions
Describe motion sequences with temporal connectors (then, next, suddenly)
Consider object permanence - re-mention items that leave and return to frame

3. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Duration: 5 seconds maximum per generation
Aspect Ratios: Multiple options (16, 9, 1)
Motion Levels: --motion low (controlled) vs --motion high (dynamic)
Quality Modes: Standard vs --raw for precise control
